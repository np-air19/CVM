01 — History

Built on a vision. Proven across generations.

In 1945, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel imagined an institution where students wouldn't just earn degrees — they'd lead industries, build companies, and shape society.

Eight decades later, CVM University is the proof. 22 colleges. 2,500+ faculty. 22,000+ students. And a national reputation GSIRF has officially recognized.

What started as one man's vision is now your launchpad.
